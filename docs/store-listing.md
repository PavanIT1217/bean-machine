## Full description

Discover how order emerges from randomness.

Galton is a beautiful, interactive bean machine. Balls tumble through a honeycomb of pegs, bounce left or
right at every row, and pile up in bins at the bottom — and, ball by ball, a perfect bell curve appears.

• Real physics: every ball bounces off the pegs and the other balls
• Pascal's triangle on the board shows how many paths lead to each spot
• Live bell curve and histogram compare what you see with the theory
• Statistics: mean, standard deviation and fit, observed vs expected
• Change the rows (5–30), bounciness, gravity, speed and even bias the board left or right
• Fast mode drops up to a million balls instantly
• Learn tab explains the binomial distribution and the central limit theorem in plain language
• Light and dark themes, works offline, no account, no ads, no tracking

Perfect for students, teachers and anyone curious about probability.
