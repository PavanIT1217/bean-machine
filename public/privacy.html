Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Galton (also published as "Bean Machine") is a probability simulator that runs entirely on your device.

  • We do not collect, store or share any personal data.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Your theme preference is saved locally on your device only.
  • The app makes no network requests other than loading itself (web version).

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/PavanIT1217/bean-machine.
